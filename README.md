## About Drift

Drift is a quiet place for the books, movies, places, ideas, experiences, and curiosities that catch your attention but don’t belong on a traditional to-do list.

Instead of letting those things disappear into a graveyard of bookmarks, Drift helps them find their way back when the moment is right.

## Taking shape

Drift is currently in early development.

The first foundations are being built thoughtfully, with an emphasis on privacy, intentional discovery, and technology that serves human curiosity without turning it into another obligation.
